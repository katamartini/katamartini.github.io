NFL droughts: last season and completed seasons without each milestone

Seasons use the NFL season year: a Super Bowl played in February 2026 belongs to the 2025 season. The ongoing season is not counted until its Super Bowl is complete. A result in the latest completed season has a drought of 0.

“Never” counts active seasons in the Super Bowl era, starting in 1966 or the franchise’s first season. Conference titles mean a Super Bowl berth, including the NFL/AFL champions in 1966–69. Earlier league championships are not Super Bowl wins. Division titles include former divisions. Franchise moves and name changes stay with the same team; the Browns’ 1996–98 suspension is excluded, and the Ravens have their own history.

Sources: nflverse game results, ESPN standings, and historical title records. Records are checked weekly and advance after a season finishes.

Regular season only. Wins carry across seasons, byes, and franchise moves; losses and ties reset the current winning streak. Playoff and preseason games are excluded. Select any threshold from 1+ to 23+. The upper limit is the Colts’ NFL record of 23 consecutive regular-season wins in 2008–09. Every further win in a qualifying run resets its game drought to 0. Green rows have an active streak at or above the selected threshold; yellow rows have exactly one fewer win and are one victory away. Pink rows have never had a qualifying streak; current green/yellow status takes priority.

“Last N+ win” is the most recent victory that left the team on a streak of at least N wins, not the first win of the run or the following loss. Games since counts only that franchise’s completed regular-season games after that victory. Time since is calendar time through the latest game date in the feed; it still passes during offseasons and inactive years. Calendar durations show whole years and months, or days for less than a month.

Coverage begins in 1946 or the franchise’s first season if later, and includes AFL and AAFC regular-season games. The historical archive is checked against every current franchise’s expected season schedule length, including strike-shortened seasons. Games before 1946 are not included. “Never” is used only when the covered results extend back to the franchise’s first playing season. In longest-drought sorts, these teams come first, ordered by first season (oldest franchise first). Their game and time droughts run from their first game. Incomplete histories remain “Not found in covered history,” have no exact drought length or last date, and sort last. Team-name and current-streak sorts follow their selected columns normally.

The current season is included. All 23 thresholds are checked weekly by the existing Tuesday drought updater. Sources: FiveThirtyEight’s historical game archive (1946–98, adapted to current franchise identities; license and attribution) and nflverse results (1999 onward). Older records show the team’s season game number, not an NFL week, because the archive has no week field. Relocations stay with the franchise; the Oilers belong to the Titans, not the Texans. Defunct clubs remain separate opponents. Cleveland played no games in 1996–98, and the Ravens have their own history.
