NFL droughts: last season and completed seasons without each milestone

Seasons use the NFL season year: a Super Bowl played in February 2026 belongs to the 2025 season. The ongoing season is not counted until its Super Bowl is complete. A result in the latest completed season has a drought of 0.

“Never” counts active seasons in the Super Bowl era, starting in 1966 or the franchise’s first season. Conference titles mean a Super Bowl berth, including the NFL/AFL champions in 1966–69. Earlier league championships are not Super Bowl wins. Division titles include former divisions. Franchise moves and name changes stay with the same team; the Browns’ 1996–98 suspension is excluded, and the Ravens have their own history.

Sources: nflverse game results, ESPN standings, and historical title records. Records are checked weekly and advance after a season finishes.
