What makes the list?

  • Team runs: at least 5 straight wins or losses, overall or at home/on the road. Winless runs of 5+ also qualify when they include ties.
  • Matchup droughts: at least 5 meetings without a win against an opponent, overall or at that venue. Neutral sites count only toward the overall matchup.
  • Player streaks: 16+ consecutive QB starts for one team; 50+ straight appearances with a catch; 20+ made field goals or 40+ made field goals and extra points combined.
  • Milestone droughts: a team one win away from its first five-win run in 10+ seasons; postseason achievements missed for at least 5 completed seasons, when that round’s game can end the drought.

Team and player streaks count regular-season games and carry across seasons. Receiving streaks count appearances; missed games preserve the run. Player watches are conditional on playing or starting, and current roster membership includes reserve players. Home/away runs skip neutral-site games.

The weekly view uses the next unfinished schedule week and shows remaining games. “Ended this season” uses the NFL season, including its January/February playoffs. A tie ends a winning or losing streak; it preserves a winless drought. Playoff-appearance droughts end when a team takes the field in the postseason. Results update every six hours and after player-feed refreshes.

Sources: nflverse schedules and results, the site’s verified QB starters, receiving and kicking feeds, and franchise milestone history. Overall team runs use the verified 1920–1998 archive plus nflverse; venue and matchup coverage starts in 1999. Player coverage dates appear below the list.
