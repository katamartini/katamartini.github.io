Tenure is measured by consecutive seasons listed with the current franchise, including the current season. Active, practice-squad and reserve players are eligible; retired and released players are excluded from the current roster. Reserve and practice-squad seasons count toward tenure.

A missing season or a season with another franchise resets the count. Brief releases and re-signings within the same franchise do not. Players with the same starting season share the lead; this is a season-based comparison, so exact signing dates do not break ties. Franchise moves stay with the same team.
