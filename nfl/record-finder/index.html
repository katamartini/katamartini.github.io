A 3–2 start means the record after exactly five regular-season games, regardless of win/loss order. Byes do not count; ties do. Postseason games do not change the starting or final regular-season record. A 0–0 search includes every eligible season. Final win percentage counts a tie as half a win.

Historical coverage follows today’s 32 franchises back to their league debuts, including earlier names/locations and AAFC/AFL history. Defunct franchises are not listed separately. Shared wartime teams count toward both participating franchises, so pooled rows are franchise-seasons, not always distinct historical clubs. The archive treats the 1932 standings tiebreaker as a regular-season game.

All outcome percentages exclude unfinished seasons, even after the regular season ends. A season enters the completed sample once its Super Bowl is final. Playoff appearance/win rates use seasons from 1933 onward; earlier seasons had no scheduled postseason and are excluded from those denominators. Super Bowl appearance/win rates use only seasons from 1966 onward. Counts and denominators are shown; “—” means no eligible sample, not 0%. Earlier playoff rounds are labeled generically rather than assigned modern round names.

“17-game era” means 2021 onward, including the 2022 Bills and Bengals seasons with 16 completed games after their canceled matchup. Strike-shortened seasons retain their actual lengths; replacement-player games count. Older seasons sometimes used unequal schedules. Current-season comparisons use only earlier completed seasons for the same franchise and the exact same record after the same number of games. These historical rates describe the sample; they are not predictions.
