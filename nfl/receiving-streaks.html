Receiving streaks

Consecutive regular-season games played with at least one catch. Games missed do not break a streak; a game played with zero catches does. Streaks carry across seasons and team changes.

Partial historical coverage: these are the top 100 within the available data, not a verified complete all-time top 100. Older published records supplement weekly box scores from 1999 onward; participation data begins in 2013.

Regular season only; preseason and playoff games are excluded. A catch counts regardless of receiving yards. Participation includes offense, defense, or special-teams snaps. Injuries, byes, suspensions, and other games not played do not reset a streak.

Before 2013, a missing box-score row cannot reliably distinguish a missed game from a zero-catch appearance. Those gaps split runs conservatively and produce ≥ minimums; multiple recorded segments may belong to one longer historical run. “Coverage gap” is not a confirmed zero-catch game. “Observed start” means the full streak may have begun earlier. Missing older records and incomplete runs may change the true all-time ordering.

“Documented” rows have independently verified published totals, with a source link under the player’s name. Unverified dates stay blank; season ranges are not exact start dates. These records replace covered, truncated data fragments so the same historical run is not counted twice.

“Unbroken · inactive” means no later zero-catch appearance is recorded, but the player is not on a current roster. Active status includes injured/reserve and practice-squad players. Data and rosters may refresh at different times; participation can delay the newest game appearing here.
