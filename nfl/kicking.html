Only field-goal attempts count in this view; an extra-point miss does not end a field-goal streak.

Regular-season attempts only; postseason and preseason games are excluded. Streaks carry across seasons and team changes. A miss or blocked kick ends a streak. Two-point tries, fake/aborted tries with no kick, and plays nullified by penalty do not count.

Start and most recent dates are the first and last made kicks in the run. “Ended by” is the next missed or blocked attempt. “Unbroken · inactive” means no later miss is recorded, but the kicker is not on a current roster. Active roster status includes injured/reserve and practice-squad players.

Coverage starts in 1999, not the beginning of NFL history. A ≥ total is a lower bound where a start or end crosses missing attempt data. These rows show recorded makes, not an exact full-run total. Rank is based on recorded makes and stays the same when filtering.

Checked weekly on Tuesday, with a Wednesday retry. Play-by-play and roster feeds may refresh at different times; bye-week teams use their latest roster. Data: nflverse play-by-play, player names, and weekly rosters. Coverage documentation. Play-by-play attribution: nflverse/nflfastR, CC BY 4.0.
