Only field-goal attempts count in this view; an extra-point miss does not end a field-goal streak.

Near-streaks contain exactly one missed or blocked kick, with at least one make on each side. Each row joins the complete make-runs immediately before and after that miss; the next miss ends it. Neighboring near-streaks can overlap. “Made / attempts” includes the lone miss in the attempt total; the split below it shows makes before + one miss + makes after. The 20/40 cutoffs and ranks use total attempts: the flawless streak that would exist if that one kick had been made. FG and XP columns count actual makes.

Regular-season attempts only; postseason and preseason games are excluded. Streaks carry across seasons and team changes. A miss or blocked kick ends a streak. Two-point tries, fake/aborted tries with no kick, and plays nullified by penalty do not count.

Start and most recent dates are the first and last made kicks in the run. “Ended by” is the next missed or blocked attempt. “Unbroken · inactive” means no later miss is recorded, but the kicker is not on a current roster. Active roster status includes injured/reserve and practice-squad players.

Play-by-play coverage starts in 1999. Older rows are a partial archive of individually verified regular-season records, with source links under the kicker’s name. A season range is not an exact start date; unverified dates are left blank. “Documented” does not mean all older streaks have been found.

A ≥ total is a lower bound where a start or end crosses missing attempt data, or only a segment is documented. Gary Anderson’s combined row includes his perfect 1998 regular season (35 FG + 59 XP), not an exact full multi-season streak. Playoff totals are not substituted for regular-season streaks. Near-streaks use only the available attempt sequences since 1999 and never bridge a data gap; older documented flawless runs are not stitched together. Rank stays the same when filtering.
