How to read the predictor

“The year before” means the previous NFL regular season—not the previous calendar year and not including playoffs. Scoring averages use total points divided by games played, rounded to one decimal; point differential is points scored minus points allowed, divided by games played. Coach age uses September 1 of the title season as a consistent opening benchmark; tenure counts consecutive seasons with that franchise. A team that survives has only avoided a historical disqualifier; the rules do not measure how likely that team is to win.

Sources

Champions are cross-checked against the NFL’s Super Bowl archive and the Super Bowl head-coach record. Regular-season records and scoring totals come from the NFL’s historical standings, including the final 2025 standings; historical records are also checked against Pro Football Reference. The 2026 coaching field is checked against the current all-team coach table and the NFL coaching tracker. Team marks come from ESPN’s NFL team directory.
