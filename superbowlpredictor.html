How to read the predictor

“The year before” means the previous NFL regular season—not the previous calendar year and not including playoffs. A team that survives has only avoided a historical disqualifier; the rule does not measure how likely that team is to win.

Sources

Champions are cross-checked against the NFL’s Super Bowl archive. The 2026 field uses the NFL’s final 2025 standings; historical records are checked against Pro Football Reference.
